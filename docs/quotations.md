# Quotations in the examples

One example show quotes a real person. This page records exactly what is quoted, why, and how to
have it removed.

## Alan Watts — `examples/alan-watts.html`

**Who.** Alan Wilson Watts (6 January 1915 – 16 November 1973), British writer and speaker.

**What is quoted.** Eight short passages, each under 40 words, taken from talks and books that
remain in copyright. The estate's rights are unaffected by this repository's MIT licence, which
covers the code only.

| # | Passage |
|---|---|
| 1 | "The only way to make sense out of change is to plunge into it, move with it, and join the dance." |
| 2 | "You are a function of what the whole universe is doing in the same way that a wave is a function of what the whole ocean is doing." |
| 3 | "This is the real secret of life — to be completely engaged with what you are doing in the here and now. And instead of calling it work, realize it is play." |
| 4 | "To have faith is to trust yourself to the water. When you swim you don't grab hold of the water, because if you do you will sink and drown. Instead you relax, and float." |
| 5 | "You are an aperture through which the universe is looking at and exploring itself." |
| 6 | "We do not 'come into' this world; we come out of it, as leaves from a tree." |
| 7 | "It is neither careless drifting on the one hand nor fearful clinging to the past on the other." |
| 8 | Attribution card: name and dates only. |

**Why.** They demonstrate what the tool does with quotation-length text: long copy that has to
shrink to fit, in a portrait layout. The show is a demonstration of the software, not a
republication of the work.

**Attribution.** Every scene carries the speaker's name in the project title, and the show ends on
a card naming him and his dates. The landing page labels the example with his name.

**Removal.** Open an issue on this repository, or email the address in `SECURITY.md`. The example
is a single file and a single card on the landing page; it can be removed in one commit.

## What this repository does not claim

- It does not claim fair use or fair dealing as a settled matter. Whether a short quotation is
  permitted depends on jurisdiction and purpose; a lawyer decides that, not this file.
- It does not sublicense the quotations. The MIT licence in `LICENSE` applies to the code.

## If you publish a show of your own

Kinemotif will animate whatever text you paste. Text you supply — a quotation, a lyric, an
extract — remains your responsibility, and the same attribution and permission questions apply to
your show as to this example.
